## 2. The Rsqrt precision finding (main output of this revision)

First implementation used `AscendC::Rsqrt` in the vector-pipe meanSquare finalize (source SHA `2d313bb7…`). It **failed correctness at D=32768**:

| shape | V016 | V023 (Rsqrt) | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| D=32768 | PASS max_abs 4.84e-7 | **FAIL max_abs 9.94e-4** | RC=3; the edit IS exercised here |
| D=24576 | PASS max_abs 3.48e-7 | PASS max_abs 3.48e-7 | batch path, edit not exercised |

**Root cause:** `AscendC::Rsqrt` on Ascend 910B3 is an **approximate** reciprocal-sqrt. The observed error 9.94e-4 matches a relative error of about 2^-10 (9.77e-4), the signature of a 10-bit fast rsqrt instruction. It cannot meet the 1e-4 FP32 budget. MAIN-1's PRECISION_RISK note expected a 1-ulp difference; the real gap is ~1e-3, three orders larger.

**Correctness fix (API usage):** replaced `Rsqrt` with `Sqrt` and restored the precise scalar reciprocal `1.0f / GetValue(0)`. Kept the vector-pipe meanSquare (`Muls` + `Adds`) and the single V/S round trip, which are the hypothesis's performance mechanism. Fixed source SHA `66b50111a4d9f98937fe494ba13f5f078194eb0638d97bfce0cbeef6f47b3c91`. After the fix both versions PASS at D=24576 and D=32768 (max_abs 3.48e-7 / 4.84e-7, matching V016 exactly).

**Transferable rule:** any future use of `Rsqrt` (and likely `Reciprocal`) for a precision-critical denominator on 910B3 must be rejected or refined with a Newton step. The scalar `1.0f / x` is the precise reciprocal.
