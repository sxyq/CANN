## 4. Mechanism read

At `localRows=2, D=24576`:

- **batch path** (`ProcessWideFp32Batched`): 6112-element tiles, two input and two output staging slots, event-flag `MTE2_V` / `V_MTE2` / `V_MTE3` / `MTE3_V` overlap across adjacent rows and tiles.
- **CachedRows** (`ProcessWideFp32CachedRows`): 7680-element tiles, two-pass serial (pass 1 all tiles then pass 2 all tiles), full row cached in `valueFp32Buf_` (98 KB at D=24576), per-tile `SyncMTE2ToV` / `SyncVToMTE2` / `SyncVToMTE3`.

Although CachedRows reads x/residual once instead of twice, its lack of cross-tile and cross-pass overlap costs more than the saved read at this width. V016's dispatch comment ("one input read beats the batch path's re-read at that width") is confirmed at D=32768 but does **not** generalize down to D=24576. The batch path's pipelining is the dominant factor at 2 rows and mid-wide D.
