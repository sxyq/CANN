## 5. Mechanism read

The per-tile `Muls(valueLocal[col], invRms)` and its `PipeBarrier` serialized the value path five times per row at D=32768. Collapsing them into one full-row `Muls` before the loop removes four V instruction issues and four `PipeBarrier`s from the critical path. The observed ~3.5% (0.7 µs of 19 µs) exceeds the naive 5 × 0.08 µs = 0.4 µs barrier-cost estimate, indicating each per-tile barrier carried more pipeline-stall cost than assumed. Because correctness max_abs matches V016 exactly, the gain is purely reduced V-issue and barrier overhead — not a numerical change.
